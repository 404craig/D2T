# Craig's brief, 11 Oct 2026 (verbatim, for the prototype build)

Reference image: EA FC27 key art (green background with soft gradients, a wide light beam, thin lines, a dark streak bottom right). Saved at design/reference/fc27_green.png.

> Remove v2 card.
>
> Let's redo the starting/opening screen to look like this attached image. Green background with gradients and lines. Our D2T smaller logo, fully in black, large, centred vertically and horizontally. It then transitions like one of the MP4 or GIF files I've given you into the longer logo with the arrow-circle part of the logo rotating 360°, about 75% width of the screen when opened. The logo then slides upwards towards the top of the screen, and reduces to 60% width as the screen transitions to our black background, and the logo transitions through to white as the green from our new background glistens and ripples through it before stopping.
>
> [I also have an idea for the green background but don't know when to fit it in: text saying "DO YOU HAVE WHAT IT TAKES TO TAKE A TEAM FROM THE DROP TO THE TOP?" with all text in black but the words DROP and TOP inverses with a black highlight background. It would vidiorint the text with a left to right transition.]
>
> [I also have an idea to keep the homescreen with the new green background and all buttons would be black. Home Screen only, and other screens would be black with small white D2T logo top left]
>
> The main game option on the home screen should read "Pick a new club" with subtitle "Dare to dream. Win the title!".
>
> If I've started a game already and come back to the homescreen, the Pick a new club option is still there, but also a Continue option which shows the team I was previously playing with. Work if the app is better having separate page urls rather than all being on the one url.
>
> Below that will be quick stats as there are now: achievements for number of times you've beat the drop (stayed out of the bottom 3), reach the top half, top 3 finish, won league titles. Potentially use the outline shape of our player and club badges for this, subtly.
>
> Below that will be different game modes coming soon. Think of a better way to display them rather than bubbles.
>
> Borrow the bar from Tenaball homescreen which shows league, stats, how to play, account.
>
> In how to play, we'll need the game rules simplified, and an explanation on the card colours and their stats. Really simplify the objective at the top of this screen.
>
> Account might say sign in or something if you haven't already. Possibly show a small account circular logo with typical account icon at the top right of all other game screens apart from the homescreen. Account screen should be similar details to tenaball and the same idea of signing in with email + pin.
>
> Work on an animation for the team/player cards when opening from small to large it should rotate/spin horizontally as it transitions in size from small to large.
>
> Now the first game screen: pick a team. From here on in, our game logo is in white at the top left of the screen, and account is at the top right. The 3 slots should be shown in the centre of the screen, left to right and not vertically set out, with blank card outlines form our new card shape and either just a question mark and scrambled data, or fake/fuzzy football club badges which can't be made out. Something that sits as a placeholder. The bottom button then has our arrow logo and the word "Spin". When hitting spin we need an extremely convincing fruit-machine-esque vertical, extremely fast spin, which cycles through loads of team cards and slows to settle on a card in the left spot followed shortly after by the middle slot, and then the last slot. Get a fable agent to sort this animation for us as it's going to be the key USP to the whole game and cannot look cheap or clunky. It should be a motion blur at speed.
>
> Perhaps the Spin button at the bottom is coloured, and either side is a circular button - left is reload with a line-drawn version of our arrow logo in the circle, and right is home. After completing a spin, you can tap a card which makes it selected, perhaps the card ever so slightly increases in size by 5% and the other two aside reduce ever so slightly. This should be a quick transition/motion and not just skip to a new look. The spin button at the bottom changes to play. If a selected card is tapped again it uses the transition spin animation I detailed earlier to move from being a small card to the larger card front and centre. Tap again to reverse the animation.
>
> After hitting play I should be presented with my team, and the historic table. Use the tenaball table reveal animation in reverse to go from top of the league table all the way down to where the team you are finished, slowing as it gets to your result. Then do an animation to reset everyone's points and stats to zero, and the bottom button says continue.
>
> After continue, you're on the squad/tactics screen. This should be a pitch with even smaller versions of our player cards, and a suggested starting XI. Below the pitch is a link to change formation which opens a slide-up screen and has different formation selections, 3 across as small pitch cards with circular icons showing the formation on the pitch. A card can be selected and the window can be closed.
>
> Below the pitch with your starting 11 and above the fixed play/continue bar, should sit player cards for substitutes in a horizontal carousel that I can cycle through. Again, I can select, and hit again to reveal a bigger card front and centre or tap a player on the pitch to swap them around. I should also be able to drag the card to replace someone on the pitch. Think fifa/ea games for this.
>
> Perhaps the play/continue bar should now show your next fixture.
>
> When continuing to the next fixture it should first show the gameweek fixtures for everyone, then continue to play match. In a match, the main button is kick off and it immediately changes to skip to end if you want to just reveal the score. After a match the final scores are shown, and after that the league table.
>
> We then cycle through the game weeks until another transfer is allowed. When a transfer opportunity comes along the screen should show a short animation of our arrow logo/icon only spinning 360° and then dissipating outward to reveal the 3 placeholder cards. This is only an idea for the animation, but again we need to make each spin opportunity feel like the USP of the game and so we shouldn't simply land on that screen with an immediate load.
>
> The league option for this game should really be able to add friends into one table which shows the number of the four achievements from the homepage and perhaps a percentage rating which makes sense for ranking players in the league.
>
> [An option to consider later will be a Multiplayer online mode where up to 3 players can play against each other in the main classic mode (perhaps more in other modes). The game would be hosted by an admin and all 3 players would be given a team each from the same season or they would spin as normal and you have to claim which team you want to go. All 3 players would be served the same option at the same time and first come first served. The game would then wait for each player to continue at each screen in the game. Each user team would be highlighted in the fixtures, and the table. Each transfer spin would have to potentially divide the pot in 3 or work out other mechanics so as not to serve the other users the same player, and not to serve a player from the other users team.]
>
> We also need to work on game mechanics as essentially bronze and silver cards aren't going to be wanted that often. Perhaps they are more useful in different versions of the game though.
