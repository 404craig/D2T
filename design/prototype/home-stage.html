Home: the stage

Option 1, built out. Swipe left and right (or drag with the mouse) to turn the wheel: the next game rotates in from the side on a big wheel, and when you let go it snaps into place with a small bounce. A quick flick turns it on to the next game.

  • The game's name is the headline, big in its colour, with a ? beside it for that game's rules (a sheet that drags closed). The line under it says what you do.
  • Four shields across the bottom, in the record's metallic look, with that game's numbers, count first.
  • No bottom bar. League, Stats and How to play are gone for now; the account is the circle top right. The big Play button sits in the thumb zone.
  • Classic: the card fan swaps its cards on a beat, like the home screen does now.
  • Daily: today's club card (Middlesbrough 1992/93, from the game), on its own and a little larger, with a slow shine.
  • Club X: "Career mode".
  • Goal Rush: "Score exactly ?-?-?", the ball turning slowly with formations floating round it.
  • Unbeaten Run: the D2T emblem turned so its arrows point up and down, in Unbeaten's blue, behind two shirts with the swipe arrows between them, gently moving.
  • Numbers are made up. With reduced motion on, nothing moves on its own.
