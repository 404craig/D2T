Ten more club badges

Ten more ways to stand in for crests in the public version, following A, B and C. Every one is a single shared template filled from the game's own data: the club's two colours and its 3-letter code, so no two clubs ever match and none copies a real crest. Each shows on black, on a light card face, and at table size. Tap one to see it in the real game below (build 225): the club spin, a transfer roll and the season-over screen, the same moment each time.
