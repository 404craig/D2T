Share cards: every game

The Daily's new card (sent to the game) set the rules; here they are carried to every other game. The same frame: the tag text centred, no link on the picture, the band lifted with black under it so WhatsApp's caption never covers it, a soft wash of the club's or game's colour at the top. The same story: a result line with the arrow on the figures' middle, a smaller line under it, then team sheet rows with the numbers in one column and the words in the next. The same message: short lines in blocks, ending Your turn. Beat that! over the link. Every player and real finish is from the game's data; your finishes are made up for the examples. Club X

Season one is where your drafted 11 finished; from season two it's the career, with the all-time top scorer and best player. At 20 seasons the tiles run out of room (five is the most that fit), so a long career needs the climb, or the record with the last five.

Goal Rush

Option A, as asked: the total as "433 GOALS" in white, no "Bang on", a bigger pitch. Both XIs are real and add up as shown.

Unbeaten Run

No ring. The arrow is the game's hand-drawn swipe arrow (the cyan marker that follows your finger), drawn the same way. The topic sits in its own box at the top: the stat, then what it means. Four takes on the call that ended it, each with its own headline. Rooney's 208 and Salah's 193 are their career Premier League goals in the game's data.

Notes

  • Colours. The tag is always the game's colour (Classic the club's). The wash and the band are the club's colour in Classic, Daily and Dare2Dream, and the game's colour in Club X, Goal Rush and Unbeaten Run, which have no club of their own.
  • Best card. The same rule as the Daily everywhere there's a squad: the highest card, or the next highest when he's also the top scorer.
  • Goal Rush. The XI becomes a team sheet in shape order, keeper first, goals lined up in one column, the biggest in yellow. The example XI is real and does add up to 433.
  • Club X career. One row a season, up to the last five; titles in green.
  • Unbeaten Run. No players to show, so the rows are this run against your best before it.
