Share cards: every game

The Daily's new card (sent to the game) set the rules; here they are carried to every other game. The same frame: the tag text centred, no link on the picture, the band lifted with black under it so WhatsApp's caption never covers it, a soft wash of the club's or game's colour at the top. The same story: a result line with the arrow on the figures' middle, a smaller line under it, then team sheet rows with the numbers in one column and the words in the next. The same message: short lines in blocks, ending Your turn. Beat that! over the link. Every player and real finish is from the game's data; your finishes are made up for the examples. Tap a picture for full size.

Notes

  • Colours. The tag is always the game's colour (Classic the club's). The wash and the band are the club's colour in Classic, Daily and Dare2Dream, and the game's colour in Club X, Goal Rush and Unbeaten Run, which have no club of their own.
  • Best card. The same rule as the Daily everywhere there's a squad: the highest card, or the next highest when he's also the top scorer.
  • Goal Rush. The XI becomes a team sheet in shape order, keeper first, goals lined up in one column, the biggest in yellow. The example XI is real and does add up to 433.
  • Club X career. One row a season, up to the last five; titles in green.
  • Unbeaten Run. No players to show, so the rows are this run against your best before it.
