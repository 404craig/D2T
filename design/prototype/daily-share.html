Daily share: four versions

How the Daily shares on WhatsApp today (Middlesbrough 1992/93, Daily #19, 21st to 1st), and three better versions. Each card is drawn on the same 1080 x 1350 canvas the game uses, then shown in a WhatsApp chat with the message text underneath, as it arrives. Tap "full size" under a chat to see the whole image.

What changes, and why

  • The white band. The dare sat at the top of the band with the link under it, so it read low, and WhatsApp's caption box covers the bottom 15 to 20px of the picture, which clipped the link. The link goes (it is in the message already), the dare is centred in the band, and the band sits 64px up from the bottom with black below it, so nothing important is in the strip WhatsApp covers.
  • Top scorer and best card. Yes, they are the bits your mates will argue about. Both are already worked out for the summary screen (top scorer from your season's goals, best card = the highest-rated player in your final XI). On the card they go in two small tiles under The Rise. Example names here are real 1992/93 players: Paul Wilkinson (Boro's real top scorer, 14) and Eric Cantona (his 90 gold card).
  • Team colour. The Daily's own colour is white, so the tag stays white, but the club's colour comes in as a glow behind the crest, your finish and the rise in the club's text colour, and the band in the club's colour. Middlesbrough's red here; every club has its colours in the data already.
  • The message. Broken into short lines with blank lines between the three parts (the club and the result, the highlights, the dare). The date comes out of the message: WhatsApp turns "29 Oct" into a calendar link (the underline in your screenshot). It stays on the picture.
