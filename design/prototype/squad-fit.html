Squad screen: everything on one screen

Each phone is the area you actually see in Safari on a current iPhone (about 390 by 700), so the header, pitch, subs and the bottom button all have to fit with no scrolling. The glows are gone from every card; a selected card lifts, its tab turns white and a small marker sits under it. Example: Wolves 2011/12 in 4-2-3-1 with Karl Henry selected; ratings are the draft card engine.
