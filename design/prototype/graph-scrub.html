How it works

  • Touch and drag sideways anywhere on the graph. A line snaps to the nearest week, with a dot on each line.
  • The rest of the season dims past your finger, so you see the season up to that week.
  • A puts the numbers in a strip above the graph, so your finger never covers them. It shows the final week when you're not touching. B puts a small bubble over the dots instead. It's closer to the action but your thumb can hide it.
  • Up-and-down drags still scroll the page, so it never traps you.
  • Each week shows who you played and the score. History's score is the real one.
  • Let go and it stays on that week. Tap outside the graph to go back to the final week.
