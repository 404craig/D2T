Pitch cards

The small cards on the squad pitch, at real size on a phone pitch (left) and blown up 2.25 times (right). Every option keeps the full name, makes the rating bigger and makes the position easier to read. Positions get one colour each: keeper amber, defence blue, midfield green, attack red. Example XI: Wolves 2011/12 after a few signings from that season; ratings are the draft card engine.
