Mode buttons

Five ways to show Classic, the main game, then five ways to show the other modes so they read as secondary and look nothing like Classic. Shown at real phone width. Mix any Classic with any other-modes option. Dare2Dream is playable; Daily challenge, Head to head and Legends are coming soon.

Classic

The one big thing on home. Every option keeps "Pick a new club" and "Take them from the drop to the top."

Other modes

Smaller and quieter than Classic. Dare2Dream gets purple (the Baller card colour) so it is clearly a different mode, not a second Classic button.
