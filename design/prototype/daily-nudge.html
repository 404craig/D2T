Nudging people to the Daily

Screenshots of the live home (build 181) with each idea placed into the real page. Today's Daily is Bolton 1995/96 (really 20th, 29 pts). Every idea does the same thing when tapped: the wheel turns back to the Daily, so you see its card and its own Play button. Nothing starts without a second tap. Each one only shows until you've played today's Daily.

On every game (the chip)

A small chip under the logo with today's crest, shown on every game except the Daily itself. It sits in empty space under the logo, so nothing moves on any page, including Tenaball's board. The red dot means not played yet.

The chip through the day

Worth weighing

  • Classic stays the hero. None of these change Classic's art, title, shields or Spin button. A and B are on the Classic page only. C goes on every game.
  • A is the clearest but it's the biggest, and it nudges Classic's art up a little. It only suits pages with room under the shields, so it couldn't go on Tenaball.
  • B is the subtlest. The Daily is the game to the left of Classic anyway, so its arrow becomes today's crest. It could pulse gently every few seconds.
  • C works anywhere and could also do A's job on Classic. My pick is C on every game, plus A on Classic only while the Daily hasn't been played. Once it's done, A goes and the chip shows "Done".
  • Once it's done the chip can either show "Done" with a tick (as below) or disappear until tomorrow. Disappearing is cleaner. "Done" tells people there's one a day.
