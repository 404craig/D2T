Share cards for every game

Your Burnley card kept as it is, plus one for each of the other games in the same frame: the long logo, the game's tag in its colour, the crest or mark faded behind, the result, and a new band at the foot that dares whoever you send it to. The message under each image is tidied to four or five short lines and ends with the challenge and the link. Each is shown as it lands in a chat.

THE CHALLENGEEach game dares the reader to beat the number that matters: your Rise in Classic (their club is different), your finish in the Daily (same club for everyone today), their club's real finish in Dare2Dream, the title in Club X, the exact target in Goal Rush, your run in Unbeaten Run.
THE MESSAGENo banter line in the text any more (it stays on the image), no "History:" and "Mine:" labels: one sentence for what you did, one line for the number, one line to dare them, then the link.
CLUB X CAREERSThe card uses your club's name and counts your seasons. One season: what you did that season (champions, or your finish). Two or more: one box per season with its finish, titles in green, and the line "2 titles in 3 seasons"; from five seasons the boxes show the last five and the line adds the rest ("4 titles in 7 seasons"). A forecast season (after 2025/26) is marked Forecast. If you went down, the line ends with it: "Relegated in season 4, after 2 titles". The dare is to beat your titles.
WHAT IS REALBurnley 2021/22 is your real result. The clubs' real finishes are checked against the results (Watford 20th in 1999/00, Everton 4th in 2004/05, Bradford 20th in 2000/01) and the Goal Rush goals are each player's real career total. The other results are made up to show the layout.
