The big player card

Three ways to set out everything we know about a player under a smaller card, tapped from a roll. All three use the live card (about two thirds of its size now) and Stuart Pearce's real numbers: his 2000/01 season with West Ham, his positions and nationality, his Premier League career and the clubs he played for, season by season. A splits it into tabs, B stacks it in labelled sections you scroll, C puts the card beside the key facts and lists his seasons.
