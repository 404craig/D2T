Small moves, real game

Six small animations for places that are still static, each recorded in the real game (build 194) with the motion added. They reuse what the game already does: rolling numbers like Goal Rush, the logo's glint, and the drop-in with a bounce the cards use. Each plays once when the thing first appears, then stays still. The clips loop so you can watch them.

Rules they all keep

  • Once, then still. Each plays when the element first appears and never loops in the game. The only ongoing motion is the progress bar's slow glow.
  • Short. Every one is over within about 1.5 seconds and never holds up a tap. Tapping during one jumps it to the end.
  • Club colour inside a run, silver, green or the game's colour outside it. No new colours.
  • Reduced motion: the numbers and tags just appear, with no roll, bounce or glint.
  • The record numbers on home and the rating rise are examples. The Leicester season is a real run of the game.
