Unbeaten Run club cards

Club stats on the Classic club-pick shield, bigger and two to a row. Both cards reel in and land with the club spin's motion, impact, light sweep and glow. Tap Higher or Lower to play: the number rolls up, the known card slides across, the next club reels in. Crest top right and the stat in the middle on all three. The 128px crests are shown at 50 to 64px with a thin light edge so a red crest still reads on a red card, so they stay sharp. Points are real and come from every result in the data (Man City's 100 in 2017/18, Derby's 11 in 2007/08 and so on); a club that went down shows as plainly as one that won it.
