Transfer News, five new looks

Today's bar is a full-width yellow strip: the one colour on the screen that isn't the club's. These five bring it into line with the rest of the game. Each is placed into the real squad screen (Club X, pre-season), centred in the gap as now, showing a rumour and then the done deal. The words still type in letter by letter as today.

F is your mix

  • The dots are yellow to match the label. They could be the club's colour instead, as in A.

My view of the first five

  • C fits best: the shield and the three small shields use the game's own shapes, the buying club's crest says who it is at a glance, and the count becomes something you can see.
  • B is the boldest and matches the Daily bar, but a white pill on the squad screen may fight the Start button for attention.
  • D is the smallest change if you like today's bar and just want it in the club's colour.
  • Whichever you pick, the deal moment can add one glint, as the other new animations do.
