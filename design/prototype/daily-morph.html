The Daily, by morph

Two ways for the top bar to suggest today's Daily, now and then, only if you haven't played it yet. Both are rebuilt over screenshots of the live home (build 181). Today's Daily is Bolton 1995/96. Tap the white pill or the crest and the wheel turns to the Daily. Each phone loops so you can watch it.

1 · The buttons merge

Share and ? slide into the account circle. The one circle turns white and stretches into a pill: Bolton's crest, "Today's Daily", Play. A glint runs across it, then it splits back into the three buttons.

2 · The logo turns

The emblem flips over to Bolton's crest on a white disc, as a glint wipes the wordmark away and writes "Play today's Daily, Bolton 95/96" in its place. Then it flips back to DROP2TOP.

How it would behave

  • Only until you play. Once you've started today's Daily it never shows again that day. No "carry on", no "done".
  • Now and then. About 4 seconds after you land on any game except the Daily, then every 30 seconds or so, three times a visit at most. It holds for about 4 seconds. Never while you're swiping the wheel.
  • Tap while it shows and the wheel turns to the Daily, where you press Play. Its own button never starts a game.
  • 1 is louder: a white pill is the brightest thing in the top bar. 2 is more on brand: it uses the emblem turn and glint the logo already has, so it could take the place of that beat.
  • Reduced motion: no flip, slide or glint. It fades from one to the other.
