What the cards say

Five tones for the main line and the line under it, on the real card style, one phone each. The daily's main line stays the club and season (it changes every day), so only its line under changes. Once you have played a mode, a small line in its colour adds your record (shown on 433 and Club X), so the description itself can stay a pitch.

WHAT EACH ONE DOES BESTPlain tells a first-timer exactly what to do. Dare and Question make people want to try. Short is the cleanest on a phone. Banter fits a 15 to 20 audience but wears thin if every card jokes, so it could be saved for one or two.
MIXINGTones can be mixed per card: for example the Question for Dare2Dream ("Could you do better?"), the Banter for 433 ("433. Not 432. Not 434."), the Dare for Club X ("From nowhere to champions").
YOUR RECORDToday the line under is replaced by your record once you have played (for example "Your best: 45 away."). With a separate small record line, the pitch stays and the record adds to it: "Best: 45 away" or "1 title from 4 goes".
