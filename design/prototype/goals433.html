433 Goals

A mockup of the 433 Goals mode: fill a 4-3-3 one shirt at a time from three spun players, and see how close your XI's Premier League career goals get to 433. Shirts are in each player's club colours (the club he scored most Premier League goals for) with his surname on the back. The goal number stays hidden until you place him. All names, clubs and goals are real (every Premier League player with 60+ games); the rules here are my reading of your brief, so treat the target and turn order as placeholders.

Pitch options

Each one looks different from the Classic pitch and says the mode on the pitch itself. The mode gets its own colour, orange, as Classic uses your club's colour and Dare2Dream uses purple.

A turn, step by step

Ready, spinning, picking, then the reveal. Shown on pitch option 1.

Try it

Tap Spin, tap a shirt to pick it (it grows, the others shrink), then tap it again or drag it onto the lit shirt on the pitch. Respin on the left, home on the right. Cycle the reveal style with the switch above the phone.

Reveal options

What happens when a shirt lands on the pitch. Each panel loops.

Goal tally options

The running total and how far it is from 433.
