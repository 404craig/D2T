Purple and legend cards: the reveal

A mock-up of the roll with the live cards. Pick where the rare card lands and whether it is purple or a legend, then hit Spin on the phone. The cards are real 2003/04 cards from the game: Thierry Henry (legend, 99) and Frank Lampard (purple, 93), with real bronze, silver and gold cards in the other slots.

Hit Spin to roll three players
  1. The three reels spin as now. The rare card's reel never lands on its own: the reels before it land in turn, and it keeps rolling, blurred, so nothing gives it away.
  2. When its turn comes (straight away in slot 1, just after the card before it lands in slots 2 and 3), the screen dims, there is a flash and a ring, and a big blank card bursts out of its slot to the middle in its level's colours. The words LEGEND or PURPLE light up over it.
  3. The six stats pop in one by one: each bursts large over the card, then shrinks onto its place.
  4. At the same time the rating rolls upwards with motion blur and slows onto his number.
  5. The name prints letter by letter while the crest and flag burst on, each with sparks.
  6. The full card holds for 2 seconds, then flies back down into its slot and lands with the usual hit and sweep. Any reels still rolling then land in turn.
  7. Every rare card plays it, every time. With reduced motion it just lands.
