Purple and legend cards: the reveal, v3

A mock-up of the roll with the live cards. Pick where the rare card lands and whether it is purple or a legend, then hit Spin on the phone. The cards are real 2003/04 cards from the game: Thierry Henry (legend, 99) and Frank Lampard (purple, 93), with real bronze, silver and gold cards in the other slots.

Who comes in?

Roll 1 of 17 this season, before GW1. Sign one or pass.

Hit Spin to roll three players
  1. The rare card's reel rolls on and slows for about a second before anything happens, then the screen darkens over it.
  2. Glass smashes out of the centre: three times the pieces, twice as long, with long splinters and a spray of tiny bits. The shards turn in 3D, catch the light on their edges and carry the card's colours. Your glass smash plays at about half the level it was (as recorded, a little duller or duller: the buttons above; tap one to hear it). Half a second in, the crowd cheers, louder, with your fireworks louder too, and both carry on through the whole reveal until the card is back in its slot, then fade.
  3. Half a second after the glass smashes, the big card fades in from small to large in the very centre. The labels (GOL, IMP and so on) are already on it.
  4. The six numbers pop in one after the other under their labels, 120% then 100%, a little slower than v2. Each arrives with a reverse swoosh and lands with a dull thud.
  5. The rating rolls with the higher numbers above, dropping into place, ticking like the reels as each passes, then slows, ticks in and lands with a thud. It fades out above and below.
  6. The flag bursts on, twice as long as v2.
  7. The crest explodes in: light rays turning behind it, two shockwaves, its own glass, a spin and a flare, three times as long, with a thud as it lands.
  8. The name appears letter by letter, 1.5 times slower, a dull tick per letter.
  9. It holds for 3 seconds, then shrinks down into its slot. The other reels carry on, or the roll is done.
