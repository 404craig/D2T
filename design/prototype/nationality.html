Nationality on the cards

We already have every player's nationality in the checked player data, so this is a design job, not a data one. Below are three places for a flag, each on the real cards at every size the game uses. The players and their seasons are real; the flags are drawn images.

THE DATAEvery one of the 18,652 player rows has a nationality, from the same two-source player file as the rest of the game: the country he played for (birth country if never capped), one per player across his whole career. 123 countries.
THE FLAGSEmoji flags break on Windows and many Android phones (England and Scotland especially, a Tenaball lesson), so flags are images: the open flag-icons set (MIT licence) covers 122 of the 123, including England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Yugoslavia needs drawing (three stripes).
THE WEIGHTAs drawn files the 122 flags are 850 KB (Spain, Mexico, Serbia carry detailed coats of arms). Shrunk to small pictures at card size they come to about 150 KB in the page, loaded once.
TO TIDYTwo spellings of one country ("Saint Kitts and Nevis" and "St Kitts and Nevis") point to the same flag. At 50px the smallest flags are about 12 px wide: clear for most, but similar flags (Ireland and Ivory Coast, Romania and Chad) can look alike, so the name stays on the big card.
