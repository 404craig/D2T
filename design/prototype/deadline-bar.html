Transfer window bar: deadline day

The window bar on the transfer screen from build 138 (Portsmouth 2009/10, first summer roll), redone in deadline-day yellow and black. Same place, same height budget, the rest of the screen untouched. The window's three segments in the season track above turn yellow too. Flip to the window's last roll to see the deadline state.

A · Breaking bar

An edge-to-edge yellow strip like a breaking-news graphic: a black slanted tag, the window's name big in black, the roll count in a black box. On the last roll the tag reads DEADLINE and blinks, and a black line drains along the bottom.
