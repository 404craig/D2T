Home (the run menu) on the left, the round respin or back button beside it, and the main button filling the rest on the right, under the thumb. Three screens below, each as it would be, with today's bar underneath to compare.

WHY IT READS BETTERThe button you press most sits on the right, where a right thumb rests, and it is the biggest thing on the bar. The two small round buttons sit together on the left, so the bar has one clear action and two quiet ones.
THE MIDDLE BUTTONIt changes with the screen: a respin with its count where a respin is allowed, a back arrow where you can go back, and the same button greyed out where neither applies, so the bar never jumps about.
EVERYWHEREThe same order would apply to every screen with a bar: rolls, squad, match, full time, table, summary, Goal Rush and Unbeaten Run.
