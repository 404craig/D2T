My view

  • C now looks most at home next to the cards: the shaded top band and the pinstripes match the club card faces.
  • B is still the clearest at table size; the rim and gloss make it read as a badge rather than a sticker.
  • A gains the most from the depth but clubs with the same colours still look the same, so it needs the name beside it.
  • The rim is the same silver for every club so the badge stands out on a card face of its own colour.
