Goal Rush

433 Goals becomes Goal Rush, in yellow. Each game picks a formation (442, 433, 451, 352, 343, 424, 532 or 541): that number is the target, and the pitch takes that shape. On home it sits where 433 Goals was. Then the game: the target reveal, a 3-5-2 game in progress, and a 4-4-2 to show the pitch changing shape. Players, clubs and career goals are real.
