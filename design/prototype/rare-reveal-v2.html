Purple and legend cards: the reveal, v2

A mock-up of the roll with the live cards. Pick where the rare card lands and whether it is purple or a legend, then hit Spin on the phone. The cards are real 2003/04 cards from the game: Thierry Henry (legend, 99) and Frank Lampard (purple, 93), with real bronze, silver and gold cards in the other slots.

Roll 1 of 17 this season, before GW1. Sign one or pass.

Hit Spin to roll three players
  1. The reels spin as now; the rare card's reel keeps rolling, blurred, until its turn.
  2. The screen goes dark. Glass shards in the card's colours explode from the centre of the screen in every direction.
  3. The big blank card bursts out of the very centre of the screen, to 115% and back.
  4. The six stats pop in one after the other, each in its own place: from nothing to 120% and back to 100%.
  5. After the sixth, the rating rolls upwards like a fruit machine, blurred while fast, then ticks in slowly over the last five numbers. Its window fades out to nothing above and below, with no hard edges.
  6. The flag bursts on in place (to 120%, back to 100%), then the crest the same way, each with a few shards.
  7. The name appears letter by letter.
  8. The card holds for 3 seconds, then shrinks from its full size down into its place in the reel. The other reels then carry on; if it was the last, the roll is done.
