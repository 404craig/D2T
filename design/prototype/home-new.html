Home: five entirely different looks

I looked at how the top game apps build their home screens, and took one different idea from each. Every option is still DROP2TOP: black, Exo 2, the green, each game in its own colour, the real crests, club cards and marks, the bottom bar, and Classic as the main game. They all work: scroll, swipe, and tap the arrows. Numbers are made up.

What I looked at: Clash Royale's single Battle button home (GameGrin), Brawl Stars' centre stage and Play button (Samurai Gamers), the NYT Games hub of daily games (Nieman Lab), Duolingo's path (Duolingo), Apple's new Games app with its carousel and Continue Playing (MacRumors), and the general rule from game UI guides: status at the top, the main action in the thumb zone, a bottom bar (guide).

How they differ

  • 1 Stage is the most like a console game: one thing at a time, no scrolling at all.
  • 2 Hub is the calmest and the quickest to read: every game on one screen with your status for today.
  • 3 Road is the most DROP2TOP: the name becomes the screen, from the drop at the bottom to the top. Your record is the road itself.
  • 4 Shelves is the most like an app store: featured at the top, carry on where you left off, then shelves.
  • 5 Reels is the boldest: each game full screen, swipe up for the next, like TikTok.
