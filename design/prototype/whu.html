Worth knowing

  • Claret is too dark to read on black. It only reaches 2.2 to 1 against black, and the game's rule is 4.5 to 1 for any club text. So A needs a lighter claret for text (5.7 to 1), and B uses the sky blue.
  • Glows are weaker in claret: the card's edge, the active season bar segment and the buttons glow far less than the blue.
  • Aston Villa and Burnley are also sky blue in the game for the same reason. If West Ham go claret, they probably should too, so the three claret-and-blue clubs match.
  • The badge sits well on all three. Its claret and gold read more strongly on the blue card. On claret it blends into the background a little more.
