DROP2TOP GAME MODES
As of build 45 (12 October 2026)


CLASSIC
They went down. How high can you take them?
Spin three real Premier League clubs that were relegated and pick one. Set a formation and your best XI from that season's real squad, then replay their 38 games while every other result stays as history played it. Through the season you get 17 transfer rolls: three player cards from the same season, sign one or pass, with 5 respins. Signing a player weakens the club you took him from. Beat the drop, reach the top half, the top 3, or win the league.

DAILY CHALLENGE
One club a day, the same for everyone.
Each day picks a club from the bottom 10 of a real season. If they really went down, it plays as Classic: can you keep them up? If they stayed up, it plays as Dare2Dream: can you beat their real finish from zero? No respins, one go, and it closes at midnight. Your result is set against your best Daily, ready to share with your mates.

DARE2DREAM
Rewrite their season.
Any club from any season, but you start with no players. Pick a formation, then draft your XI: 11 spins, one per shirt, keeper first, three players for that position each time, no respins. Then play the season with transfer rolls and 5 respins. Finish higher than they really did, or win the league again if they won it.

CLUB X
From nowhere to champions.
Spin three seasons and pick one. Club X, a made-up club in D2T green, takes the place of that season's bottom club with no players. Draft your XI the Dare2Dream way, then play the season with transfer rolls and 5 respins. Only the title counts.

433 GOALS
Score exactly 433 goals.
Fill a 4-3-3 in any order. Each spin shows three real players for your open positions, drawn from the top Premier League scorers. Pick one, put him on a shirt, and his career Premier League goals roll up like a fruit machine and add to your total. Aim for an XI total of exactly 433: you're scored on how far short or over you land, and your best is kept. 3 respins a game.
