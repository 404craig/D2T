Top 50 by season, 2000 to 2010

Draft ratings learned from eight seasons of blind panel ratings, using only verified data: goals, assists, clean sheets, minutes, starts, where the club finished, awards, and the player's other Premier League seasons (two either side). One card curve for every player: ratings up to 82 are unchanged, 6 to 8 players clear 90 each season, normal peaks are 96 to 97 and only an outstanding season reaches 99. "Panel" is the blind panel's rating where that club and season were covered (2001/02, 2004/05 and 2008/09); a difference of 6 or more is in red.
