Card styles

Two questions: where the club badge goes now there are no player photos, and what colours the five card types should be. Fees are gone from every card. Tap a layout below and every card on the page redraws in it.

Question 1

Where does the club badge go?

The same two cards in each layout: a gold Gerrard and a bronze Savage, so you can see how a red badge and a white badge sit on different metals.

The badges here are plain shields in club colours with a three-letter code, drawn by the game. Real crests would drop into the same spot, but they are trademarks, so a public App Store release would need licences or exactly this kind of stand-in.

Question 2

Bronze, silver, gold

Fixed. Bronze under 65, silver 65 to 74, gold 75 and up. Every card has the lab's faded top border and a soft shift of colour across it: bronze runs copper to rose, silver cool white to lavender, gold lemon to amber. Only the special cards get the thin pinstripe inside the border.

Question 3

Club's best player

One card per club per season: the highest rated player in that squad. Each option shows Ronaldo (Man Utd, 93) and Kenny Miller (Derby, 66), because a club's best can be a superstar or the one decent player in a relegated side. The card has to make both feel special.

Notes

What to decide

Layout

B or C makes the rating the hero, which suits a game where the rating is the decision. A wastes the middle of the card when the badge is a plain shield. C keeps the club in the picture without spending space on it, but the faded badge can muddy light cards (look at silver and platinum).

Club's best

Purple reads as special straight away and never clashes with a metal. Club colours are the most personal (a Derby fan gets a black and white card), but some clubs look like each other and a white club card can look like silver. An outline or a "Club's best" label helps whichever is chosen.

Legends

Platinum with a shimmer looks rarest, but at a glance it is close to silver, so it needs the moving shimmer to work. Red is bold and easy to tell apart, but Man Utd, Liverpool and Arsenal fans may read it as a club colour. Black and gold is unmistakable and works on any phone screen.

Who is a legend?

Options from data we have or will have: a player's best season when his career passes a bar (300 PL apps, 100 goals or 100 clean sheets); every PFA or Football Writers' Player of the Year season; or a hand-picked list. Same player can still have ordinary cards for his other seasons.
