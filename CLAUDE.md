# DROP2TOP: notes for Claude Code

A mobile web football game. The page is built: edit `src/` and `data/`, then run `python3 scripts/build-data.py` (if data changed) and `python3 scripts/build.py`. Never edit `index.html` by hand. Bump `version.txt` for every change people will notice; Home Screen copies compare `version.txt` with `D2T_BUILD` and reload themselves (only on screens where nothing is lost).

## How the game works (src/engine.js)

- Pure and deterministic: every random draw comes from `stream(seed, purpose, step)`, so the same seed and choices replay identically. No clock, DOM or storage in the engine.
- A run: club roll (3 relegated club-seasons, respins cost one of 5) → formation and XI from the real squad (the rest is forgotten) → 17 rolls: 3 pre-season, 1 every 3 games, 3 before the club's first January match, then 1 every 3 games (`schedule`).
- Each roll is three cards from the same season (10+ league games, never your own squad, never a player seen before in the run). Levels (Craig, 11 Oct): bronze under 70, silver 70 to 79, gold 80+, purple = the season's top 5 players league-wide, legend = the top 3 players of every season (102 cards, rarer than purple; ids `L:<name>:<season>`, older saved runs' `L:<name>` resolve to his best legend card). Odds in `RULES.levelOdds`, balanced with `node tests/calibrate-all.js` (nudged on 13 Oct with the match refit, purple 2.5% and gold 13%, so greedy runs still win the title about 1 in 80). Within a level every eligible player is as likely as the next, so how often one man turns up follows his level's odds over its pool: across 200 seeds a given bronze player (about 100 a season) is offered in about 27% of runs, a purple (5) in about 25%, a gold (about 55) in 11% and a silver (about 220) in 8%; nobody is offered in more than about 40% of runs. Anti-repeat (Craig, 13 Oct: a club-season must never serve the same players run after run, "flexible, variable and interesting"): the app remembers the cards each of the last three runs of a club-season put on the table (`state.recent[season:club]`, per phone, newest first, 3 runs a key, 40 keys; `GAME.rememberOffered` once a run is recorded or dropped for another, `GAME.recentFor` at `chooseClub` and a career's `nextSeason`) and the engine draws them at a tenth, a quarter and half the chance of a fresh card within their level (`RULES.recent`, `E.recentWeights`, `st.recent` kept on the run so a seed and its history replay the same, `st.offered` every card every roll served; the engine never reads storage, the history is an input). Level odds are untouched. Measured on 10 consecutive greedy runs: Derby 2007/08 consecutive runs share 20.5% of their cards without the memory and 5.2% with it (Derby 2001/02: 27.2% to 11.1%); the bronze pool is small (64 to 95 a season with 10+ games, 26 bronze cards a run), so across 10 runs the distinct count only rises from 258 to 272 of 510 (2007/08). Balance with the memory (`HIST=1 node tests/calibrate-all.js 12`): greedy title 1.1%, top 4 11.9%, top half 63.1%, stay up 93.4%, 53 pts (without: 1.2%, 12.5%, 63.7%, 93.7%, 53).
- January movers: one card with both clubs, in the order he played for them; `m2` is his first game for the second club (from the match line-ups, `data/source/moves.json`). The card shows your club if he is in your squad, otherwise the club he was at on the current match date (`E.clubAt`), and signing him raids that club.
- Positions: each player-season has preferred positions, main first (`p`, e.g. `["CM","CAM","CDM"]`, from `data/source/positions.json`); the broad GK/DEF/MID/FWD (`b`) stays for the draft, stats and players without detail. Fit: his main position green at 100%, his other preferred positions green at 95% (`RULES.fit.alt`); every other shirt takes the best colour any of his positions gives it in `FIT` (Craig, 13 Oct, set on the Position fit page claude.ai/artifact/Xod2g9hMPi9QHxqnkCtjWD): stay in your lane, so a central player never goes amber out wide and a wide player never goes amber in the middle or on the other side; LB gives LWB green (95%) and LM, LW amber, RB the mirror; CB gives only CDM amber; LM gives LWB, LW and CM amber, RM the mirror. Amber is 90%, anything else red at 70%. Pearce (CB, LB): green CB, LB, LWB; amber CDM, LM, LW; red on the right. Salah (RW, ST): green RW, ST; amber RM, LW, CAM. Cards show the main position; the big card lists the others. While a card is selected or dragged on the squad or transfer pitch (and the Club X draft), every shirt gets a 1px rounded ring in green, amber or red for him 3.5px out from it (`se-pf-*`, `--ring`; Craig, 13 Oct, `design/prototype/fit-ring-glow.css`, SVG masks drawn 1:1 for the 50 x 65 pitch card so the line is even and the point never clipped): round a card the gap inside the ring glows in the fit colour from behind it (`.se-cardwrap::before`; nothing is laid over the card), round an empty shirt (`.se-emptywrap`) the shirt is also filled faintly. A card on the pitch shows the shirt it is in on its tab, the tab coloured green (one of his positions), amber (next to them) or red (out of position) (`tab` and `cls: 'fit-…'` in `D2T.cards.player`; Craig, 13 Oct).
- Matches: Poisson goals from attack v defence and midfield gaps, fitted to every real result (`tests/fit-match.js`, `RULES.match`; each match's goals come from `stream(seed, "match", gw)`, so only the seed and the XI decide a result). The fit is Newton's method on a home and an away intercept, `att` and `mid` (13 Oct: `base`, `home` and `away` are one parameter too many, and the old gradient descent never converged, leaving 3.4 goals a game against 2.7 real and twice the real share of 5-goal margins); on real fixtures the model now gives 2.70 goals a game, 23.7% draws and 2.2% of matches won by 5 or more (real 2.69, 25.6%, 2.0%), checked in `tests/engine.test.js`. Opponents use their real squad's best XI minus anyone you've signed.
- Raids: signing a player lowers his club's strength from that date; each of its later real wins or draws can be downgraded with chance 3 x the strength lost (`otherResults`). Everyone else's results stay as history.
- Table: other clubs' matches count on their real dates; your 38 are simulated.

## Interface (src/app)

- Built from the design prototype (`design/prototype`, spec in `design/UI_SPEC.md`). `core/app.js` is the shell: `D2T.screen(name, def)`, hash routes, `D2T.state` saved as `d2t-v2`, `D2T.sheet`, `D2T.topbar`, logos as masks. `core/game.js` (`D2T.game`) is the only bridge to the engine and data: screens never call `D2TE` directly. `core/season.js` holds the in-run helpers, `core/boot.js` starts sound and the router.
- Screens: intro (green, then to black), home (black), then the small white D2T logo and the account circle: howto, account, league, stats, spin (club roll), table (historic reveal), squad (pitch, formations, drag to swap), transfer (the roll, then the pitch above a tray of the three cards), fixtures, match, summary.
- In-run screens only open when the run has reached them (`GAME.allowed`, `GAME.stepFor`); spins and the table reveal never replay (`run.spun`, `run.tableSeen`). Matches are decided in the engine before the animation, so a refresh can't change a result.
- Squad: the whole real squad is on the bench before the season and a formation change picks a new best XI; once the season starts only the XI is left. In Dare2Dream the squad screen first shows empty shirts (pick the shape, then Start the draft; before the draft its bar is home, a greyed respin and Start the draft, Craig 13 Oct); the draft runs on the transfer screen (`GAME.inDraft`, `GAME.draftInfo`, `GAME.draftPick`; engine `draftPick`, `draftSlot`, `draftFormation`).
- Transfer: when the three cards land they drop into a tray and the pitch appears above; drag a card onto a shirt (or tap it, then the shirt) to sign him there (`GAME.signAt`). The best shirt for the selected card pulses. Tap the selected card again for the big card (Craig, 13 Oct; option B in `design/prototype/player-detail.html`, `D2T.cards.info`): the card at about two thirds size (236 wide on a 393 phone), then This season (border tinted in the card's level colour) and PL career side by side, each games, goals, assists and clean sheets in the same spots for every player, then a strip with his positions (main filled in the level colour) and his seasons and club crests in career order (4, then +n). Career assists and clean sheets are summed at build time from his seasons' `as` and `cs` (`careers` in the data). The overlay scrolls when it doesn't fit (`scroll` in `openLarge`); scrolling or tapping the panels never closes it, the card, the X or outside does. The squad screen's big card is the same. Respin lifts the cards back to the middle first; the main button is Pass.
- Team rating is the average of the 11 effective ratings (each cut to 90% or 70% when out of position).
- Long surnames shrink to fit the card (`fitName` in `core/cards.js`), never an ellipsis unless at 55%.
- Accounts: optional sign-in with the same email and 4-digit PIN accounts as Tenaball, on Tenaball's server (`tenaball.crw84.workers.dev`, which allows 404craig.github.io). Runs and records stay on the phone for now.
- Achievements are cumulative (a title also counts as top 3, top half and beating the drop) and count every full season of Classic, the Daily (every go) and Club X (every career season), Craig 13 Oct (`GAME.record`, `GAME.classicRuns`). Dare2Dream runs are recorded apart (`mode` on each run, `record.dreamClubs`, `GAME.dreamRecord`). The real result is never shown after a match.
- First-time tips (Craig, 12 Oct): `D2T.tip(key, el, text)` shows a handwritten note with a curved arrow (the respin tip's style) once ever per key (`state.tips`), one at a time, gone on the next tap; `D2T.tips([...])` shows the first unseen of a list. Now: squad (shape, then a sub), a picked card on a roll (tap again for his career), match (skip to the end), Goal Rush (the target, dragging a shirt onto a yellow shirt, respins).
- Phone rules from Tenaball: tap targets 44px or more; safe-area padding; solid status bar strip (`.screen::before`); sound starts on the first tap; reduced motion respected; share image built ahead so Share opens at once; sheets drag closed.
- Build order of CSS and JS is the list at the top of `scripts/build.py`; add new screens there.
- Loading (12 Oct): the page streams in parts so the intro shows within a second on a phone: logos, the shell (`SHELL` in build.py: app.js and intro.js, which start the intro), the data (game data as a `JSON.parse` string), the rest of the game (boot.js calls `D2T.ready()`; the intro waits for it with `D2T.whenReady` before going home), then the sound clips (`D2TSound.clipsLoaded`). Nothing in the shell may need the data at load.

## Data

- `data/source/pl_results.csv` and `pl_players.csv` come from Tenaball (two sources each, see the READMEs there).
- Ratings: `data/ratings/rated_fit_*.json` from the D2T player data work, every season 1992/93 to 2025/26 (method in `docs/cards/RATINGS_TEST.md`, built by `tools/rate_v5.py` and `tools/fit_panel.py` on the player data branch). The fallback model in `build-data.py` only applies if a season's file is missing (`src: "f"`, shown as provisional).
- Preferred positions: `data/source/positions.json` from Transfermarkt's position for every Premier League game (`tools/fetch_tm_positions.py`, cache in `docs/cards/feed/tm/`), matched to Tenaball player-seasons and reduced to a main position plus up to three others by `tools/build_positions.py` and `tools/positions_rule.py`; checked against the Premier League feed's formation slots from 2015/16 (`tools/fetch_slots.py`, `tools/check_positions_feed.py`). Method and results in `docs/cards/POSITIONS.md`.
- Club accents in `data/source/clubs.json` (`accent`, `ink`, `text`); every `text` passes 4.5:1 on black.
- Crests are trademarks: fine for the family version, replace before any public release.
