# DROP2TOP: notes for Claude Code

A mobile web football game. The page is built: edit `src/` and `data/`, then run `python3 scripts/build-data.py` (if data changed) and `python3 scripts/build.py`. Never edit `index.html` by hand. Bump `version.txt` for every change people will notice; Home Screen copies compare `version.txt` with `D2T_BUILD` and reload themselves (only on screens where nothing is lost), once per build (`sessionStorage` `d2t-reloaded`) to `?v=<build>` past the cache, so a host still serving the old page for a few minutes after a release can never loop the intro (14 Oct; Safari was stuck reloading).

## Rules set by Craig (keep these)

- Never use em dashes in any text in the game or docs. British English.
- Only use data that has been checked against a source. If it can't be verified, stop rather than guess.
- Main doesn't wait for approval (Craig, 14 Oct: "main shouldn't wait for me to approve anything. If I approve it in any session then main should go ahead."): when the design session or another D2T session passes on something Craig has approved, build and release it straight away, all of it; don't hold parts back as not yet approved.
- Classic is the main mode and stays front and centre with fixed rules and few settings. Dare2Dream sits under it on home: any club from any season, start with no players, pick a shape, draft 11 (one spin per shirt, keeper first, three players of that position, no respins), then the season with no pre-season rolls and 5 respins; beat their real finish or win the league again if they won it. Drafted players leave their real clubs like signings (raids dated before the season). Daily challenge (Craig, 11 Oct): one club a day for everyone (UK date), and only the club is shared, as many goes as you like with the best go of the day counting (Craig, 13 Oct; `GAME.restartDaily`, Go again on the summary, Restart in the run menu, the card reads "Your best today: 14th. Tap to go again and beat it."), rotating through every relegated club-season, Classic's own list: a fixed shuffled cycle (`GAME.dailyCycle`, ordered by a hash of each key), Daily #1 first, one a day, then round again from the top (Craig, 13 Oct). It is a Classic run, never separate rules: their real squad and XI, Classic's 17 rolls and 5 respins. Every go, for every player, has a random seed of its own (Craig, 13 Oct: a shared seed meant everyone picked the same cards, got the same results and shared the same scores; it NEEDS to be random): built like Classic's and prefixed `daily-YYYY-MM-DD-` (`GAME.dailySeed`), kept on the run (`run.eng.seed`) so a refresh mid-go keeps its cards and results; the day's club comes from `GAME.dailyCycle` and nothing else is shared; the anti-repeat memory (below) means a second go never serves the first go's cards either; a Daily for today begun under the old rules (a draft, or another club) is dropped and starts again; numbered from Daily #1 on 11 Oct 2026. It closes at midnight (Craig, 12 Oct): an unfinished Daily from an earlier day is dropped with no result (`GAME.closeOldDaily`, on load, on home and every 30 s; home says so once). Its summary, home card and share text set today against your best Daily (`GAME.dailyCompare`, `GAME.dailyBest`: biggest rise, then higher finish). Every game ends with Share. It has its own save slot (`state.dailyRun`, and a run in progress is parked in `state.parked`), results in `record.daily`, and doesn't count towards Classic or Dare2Dream records. Sharing is text for now; a friends leaderboard needs the server. ## How the game works (src/engine.js)

- Pure and deterministic: every random draw comes from `stream(seed, purpose, step)`, so the same seed and choices replay identically. No clock, DOM or storage in the engine.
- A run: club roll (3 relegated club-seasons, respins cost one of 5) → formation and XI from the real squad (the rest is forgotten) → 17 rolls: 3 pre-season, 1 every 3 games, 3 before the club's first January match, then 1 every 3 games (`schedule`).
- The engine rethink (Craig, 18 Oct; `docs/SCOPE_ENGINE.md`, build 128; "think about the full game mechanics and build it all out"): (1) **Stars play above their number, for your XI only** (`RULES.star`, `starRating`, in `userStrength`): above 80 a purple's points count 1.5 and a legend's 2 (a gold plays at his number, so the levels matter), so an 89 purple plays as 93.5 and a 93 legend as 106; history's clubs play as they did (`clubStrength` never takes the curve) less what you took from them. The team rating on the screens is the plain average (`plainStrength`, `GAME.strength`); the matches use `userStrength` (stars, the tactic, settling), never shown as a number. (2) **Tactics** (`st.tactic`, default `mid`; `RULES.tactics`, `tacticLines`, `setTactic`, `GAME.TACTICS`): mid block (the plain model), low block, possession, counter attack, high press; a tactic leans on one of your lines measured against your own XI's average (each line's edge less `RULES.lineOffset`, the real clubs' average offsets), so it pays when that line is your strength and costs when it is your weakness; in a shape it is built for it bites in full, in any other at half (`RULES.halfShape`), so the shape never pays on its own; the counter attack costs 3 for up to +4 round your best forward. A chip bottom right of the squad and roll pitches (`SE.tacticChip`, the twin of the shape chip) opens the sheet (`SE.tacticSheet`: five boards in your shape that move the way the tactic does, one line each, the shapes it is built for, "your shape" when it is); the squad header shows your three lines as bars with the best in the accent (`SE.lineBars`, `GAME.lines`); no "Suits your XI" and no points preview, but once per run after six games with the tactic never touched the fixtures say "Your defence is your best line. A low block would lean on it." (`GAME.tacticHint`, `run.tacticHinted`). (3) **Settling in** (`settleOf` on `sign`, `settledRating`, `RULES.settle`): a signing hits the ground running (30%; purples and legends always, Craig 18 Oct), settles over two games at halfway, or takes three games from a quarter of the way up; seeded per card; never below the shirt's rating before him (60 for an empty shirt); not for draft picks, carried-in players or free agents; dots under his rating on the pitch card, one per game to go (`opts.dots` in `D2T.cards.player`, `GAME.settling`), and the roll's hint says how he settles. (4) **Raids** (`otherResults`, `lineLoss`, `RULES.raid`): a raided club's real scorelines scale by what its XI lost through the match model's own sensitivity (goals down by the attack and midfield lost, goals against up by the defence and midfield lost, seeded rounding), harder the better the opponent (1.6 minus 0.06 x his real final position, floor 0.3; m 2.5): Man Utd 2000/01 keep 29, 24, 19 of their 32 points against the top half with Keane, then Beckham, then Scholes gone (Craig's 29 / 25 / 20), 47, 42, 38 of 48 against the bottom half; no cap; a poached player's club (Club X) the reverse; nobody taken means history untouched. No real form anywhere (Craig). (5) **Scouting** (`scoutInfo`, `scout`, `scoutClubs`, `scoutClub`, `RULES.scoutCost` 2; `GAME.scout`, `GAME.scoutClub`, `respinChoice` in transfer.js): the roll's respin button opens one sheet titled "N respins left" (Craig, 20 Oct; `.se-optcard`, the tactic sheet's style, a cost pill on each) with Respin (1), Scout a position (2) and Scout a club (2). A position: your shirts drawn as the pitch's own shirts on a small pitch (`.se-scoutshirt` holding a `.se-empty`), red where nobody this season is better than the man in it (a key says so), a count on the rest; tap one for three cards green there and better than him (drawn by rating like a roll; fewer than three better are filled with the next best, "Next best" under such a card; Craig, 22 Oct, build 151: no "Better" tag); a red shirt costs nothing and names the best man there. ## Data

- `data/source/pl_results.csv` and `pl_players.csv` come from Tenaball (two sources each, see the READMEs there).
- Icons and honours (Craig, 15 Oct; `docs/cards/ICONS.md`): `data/source/honours.json` holds every season's PFA Team of the Year, PFA and FWA Player of the Year, PL Player of the Season and Golden Glove, parsed from the Wikipedia pages it names (the Golden Boot is the data's own top scorer); `data/source/research/icons_*.json` are the published all-time lists per position with their sources; `tools/build_icons.py` turns them and Craig's list into `data/source/icons.json` (consensus, verified peak seasons with evidence). Rerun it, then build-data, after changing any of them. The research files' own peak guesses are never used (from memory).
- Ratings: `data/ratings/rated_fit_*.json` from the D2T player data work, every season 1992/93 to 2025/26 (method in `docs/cards/RATINGS_TEST.md`, built by `tools/rate_v5.py` and `tools/fit_panel.py` on the player data branch). The fallback model in `build-data.py` only applies if a season's file is missing (`src: "f"`, shown as provisional).
- Preferred positions: `data/source/positions.json` from Transfermarkt's position for every Premier League game (`tools/fetch_tm_positions.py`, cache in `docs/cards/feed/tm/`), matched to Tenaball player-seasons and reduced to a main position plus up to three others by `tools/build_positions.py` and `tools/positions_rule.py`; checked against the Premier League feed's formation slots from 2015/16 (`tools/fetch_slots.py`, `tools/check_positions_feed.py`). Method and results in `docs/cards/POSITIONS.md`. Positions are a player's career, not the season's (Craig, 29 Oct: Bale is LB, LW and LM in every season): only the main position is the season's own; the others are every position Transfermarkt recorded for him in 10%+ of his recorded games or 15+ of them, up to five in all (`career_positions` in build_positions.py). Players with under 20 recorded games (mostly the 1990s, when Transfermarkt logged 8 to 30% of games) take the positions their Wikipedia page names (`data/source/positions_wiki.json`, 633 players, each with its quote and page, on the season and club cards inside his infobox spells so namesakes are untouched; fetched by `tools/fetch_wiki_positions.py`, read by Sonnet agents, every quote checked against the page). Balance with them (`HIST=1 node tests/calibrate-all.js 12`, 1,236 runs, build 187 against 183): average title 1.9% (1.6%), top 4 20.7%, top half 73.8%, 57 pts; greedy 10.8% (11.6%), top 4 51.4%, 66 pts: no change beyond noise. Craig's own calls override the data for every season of a player (`data/source/positions_overrides.json`, applied by build-data.py: a list replaces his positions, `{"add": [...]}` keeps each season's own and adds these as preferred positions straight after his main one; 15 Oct: Gianfranco Zola CAM, then ST; 22 Oct, build 149: Gareth Bale adds LW, green in all seven of his seasons and on his legend card).
- Club accents in `data/source/clubs.json` (`accent`, `ink`, `text`); every `text` passes 4.5:1 on black.
- Crests are trademarks: fine for the family version, replace before any public release.
- White crests on the club's own card (Craig, 12 and 14 Oct): where a crest is all, or nearly all, the card's colour it is shown white on every club card (Classic and Daily club spin, Unbeaten Run), via `crestCard` in `data/source/clubs.json` (a white file `<club>_white.png` in `assets/crests/72` and `128`; `crestCard` in core/game.js). The list: Nottingham Forest, Liverpool, Sheffield Wed. Checked on 14 Oct for every club (share of a crest's pixels close to its card colour, then each card looked at): Blackpool's orange ring lettering fades on the orange card but its arms read, Oldham's navy owl reads on blue, so neither is whitened.
