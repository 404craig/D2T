# DROP2TOP: notes for Claude Code

A mobile web football game. The page is built: edit `src/` and `data/`, then run `python3 scripts/build-data.py` (if data changed) and `python3 scripts/build.py`. Never edit `index.html` by hand. Bump `version.txt` for every change people will notice; Home Screen copies compare `version.txt` with `D2T_BUILD` and reload themselves (only on screens where nothing is lost), once per build (`sessionStorage` `d2t-reloaded`) to `?v=<build>` past the cache, so a host still serving the old page for a few minutes after a release can never loop the intro (14 Oct; Safari was stuck reloading).

## Rules set by Craig (keep these)

- Never use em dashes in any text in the game or docs. British English.
- Only use data that has been checked against a source. If it can't be verified, stop rather than guess.
- Main doesn't wait for approval (Craig, 14 Oct: "main shouldn't wait for me to approve anything. If I approve it in any session then main should go ahead."): when the design session or another D2T session passes on something Craig has approved, build and release it straight away, all of it; don't hold parts back as not yet approved.
- Classic is the main mode and stays front and centre with fixed rules and few settings. Dare2Dream sits under it on home: any club from any season, start with no players, pick a shape, draft 11 (one spin per shirt, keeper first, three players of that position, no respins), then the season with no pre-season rolls and 5 respins; beat their real finish or win the league again if they won it. Drafted players leave their real clubs like signings (raids dated before the season). Daily challenge (Craig, 11 Oct): one club a day for everyone (UK date), and only the club is shared, as many goes as you like with the best go of the day counting (Craig, 13 Oct; `GAME.restartDaily`, Go again on the summary, Restart in the run menu, the card reads "Your best today: 14th. Tap to go again and beat it."), rotating through every relegated club-season, Classic's own list: a fixed shuffled cycle (`GAME.dailyCycle`, ordered by a hash of each key), Daily #1 first, one a day, then round again from the top (Craig, 13 Oct). It is a Classic run, never separate rules: their real squad and XI, Classic's 17 rolls and 5 respins. Every go, for every player, has a random seed of its own (Craig, 13 Oct: a shared seed meant everyone picked the same cards, got the same results and shared the same scores; it NEEDS to be random): built like Classic's and prefixed `daily-YYYY-MM-DD-` (`GAME.dailySeed`), kept on the run (`run.eng.seed`) so a refresh mid-go keeps its cards and results; the day's club comes from `GAME.dailyCycle` and nothing else is shared; the anti-repeat memory (below) means a second go never serves the first go's cards either; a Daily for today begun under the old rules (a draft, or another club) is dropped and starts again; numbered from Daily #1 on 11 Oct 2026. It closes at midnight (Craig, 12 Oct): an unfinished Daily from an earlier day is dropped with no result (`GAME.closeOldDaily`, on load, on home and every 30 s; home says so once). Its summary, home card and share text set today against your best Daily (`GAME.dailyCompare`, `GAME.dailyBest`: biggest rise, then higher finish). Every game ends with Share. It has its own save slot (`state.dailyRun`, and a run in progress is parked in `state.parked`), results in `record.daily`, and doesn't count towards Classic or Dare2Dream records. Sharing is text for now; a friends leaderboard needs the server. Poached (Craig, 14 Oct; option B "Poached" in `design/prototype/clubx-raid.html` on the design branch): from season 2 on, on the pre-season squad before the Summer window, three of your XI are taken by other sides, never the keeper, each to a different club in the league (never yours, never his own real club that season); `E.poach`, seeded by `stream(seed, "poach", career)`, done once (`eng.poached`, `{ id, name, club, slot }`, decided as the show starts so a refresh never replays or rerolls it; `GAME.poachDue`, `GAME.poach`, also run by `GAME.startSeason` if the squad was skipped). Victims are drawn weighted by (rating / the XI's outfield average) to the 4th power, so stars are about three times as likely to go as the weakest but anyone can; clubs by their final position that season (real, or the forecast's), weight 21 minus position (the champions 20 times the bottom side). A poached player joins his new club for the season (`E.joinersOf`: it picks him against you and is stronger for it, and its other results improve the way a raid's get worse: a defeat can become a draw, a draw a win, chance 3 x the strength gained) and never comes back in your rolls (nor another card of him). ## How the game works (src/engine.js)

- Pure and deterministic: every random draw comes from `stream(seed, purpose, step)`, so the same seed and choices replay identically. No clock, DOM or storage in the engine.
- A run: club roll (3 relegated club-seasons, respins cost one of 5) → formation and XI from the real squad (the rest is forgotten) → 17 rolls: 3 pre-season, 1 every 3 games, 3 before the club's first January match, then 1 every 3 games (`schedule`).
- The engine rethink (Craig, 18 Oct; `docs/SCOPE_ENGINE.md`, build 128; "think about the full game mechanics and build it all out"): (1) **Stars play above their number, for your XI only** (`RULES.star`, `starRating`, in `userStrength`): above 80 a purple's points count 1.5 and a legend's 2 (a gold plays at his number, so the levels matter), so an 89 purple plays as 93.5 and a 93 legend as 106; history's clubs play as they did (`clubStrength` never takes the curve) less what you took from them. The team rating on the screens is the plain average (`plainStrength`, `GAME.strength`); the matches use `userStrength` (stars, the tactic, settling), never shown as a number. (2) **Tactics** (`st.tactic`, default `mid`; `RULES.tactics`, `tacticLines`, `setTactic`, `GAME.TACTICS`): mid block (the plain model), low block, possession, counter attack, high press; a tactic leans on one of your lines measured against your own XI's average (each line's edge less `RULES.lineOffset`, the real clubs' average offsets), so it pays when that line is your strength and costs when it is your weakness; in a shape it is built for it bites in full, in any other at half (`RULES.halfShape`), so the shape never pays on its own; the counter attack costs 3 for up to +4 round your best forward. A chip bottom right of the squad and roll pitches (`SE.tacticChip`, the twin of the shape chip) opens the sheet (`SE.tacticSheet`: five boards in your shape that move the way the tactic does, one line each, the shapes it is built for, "your shape" when it is); the squad header shows your three lines as bars with the best in the accent (`SE.lineBars`, `GAME.lines`); no "Suits your XI" and no points preview, but once per run after six games with the tactic never touched the fixtures say "Your defence is your best line. A low block would lean on it." (`GAME.tacticHint`, `run.tacticHinted`). (3) **Settling in** (`settleOf` on `sign`, `settledRating`, `RULES.settle`): a signing hits the ground running (30%; purples and legends always, Craig 18 Oct), settles over two games at halfway, or takes three games from a quarter of the way up; seeded per card; never below the shirt's rating before him (60 for an empty shirt); not for draft picks, carried-in players or free agents; dots under his rating on the pitch card, one per game to go (`opts.dots` in `D2T.cards.player`, `GAME.settling`), and the roll's hint says how he settles. (4) **Raids** (`otherResults`, `lineLoss`, `RULES.raid`): a raided club's real scorelines scale by what its XI lost through the match model's own sensitivity (goals down by the attack and midfield lost, goals against up by the defence and midfield lost, seeded rounding), harder the better the opponent (1.6 minus 0.06 x his real final position, floor 0.3; m 2.5): Man Utd 2000/01 keep 29, 24, 19 of their 32 points against the top half with Keane, then Beckham, then Scholes gone (Craig's 29 / 25 / 20), 47, 42, 38 of 48 against the bottom half; no cap; a poached player's club (Club X) the reverse; nobody taken means history untouched. No real form anywhere (Craig). (5) **Scouting** (`scoutInfo`, `scout`, `scoutClubs`, `scoutClub`, `RULES.scoutCost` 2; `GAME.scout`, `GAME.scoutClub`, `respinChoice` in transfer.js): the roll's respin button opens one sheet titled "N respins left" (Craig, 20 Oct; `.se-optcard`, the tactic sheet's style, a cost pill on each) with Respin (1), Scout a position (2) and Scout a club (2). A position: your shirts drawn as the pitch's own shirts on a small pitch (`.se-scoutshirt` holding a `.se-empty`), red where nobody this season is better than the man in it (a key says so), a count on the rest; tap one for three cards green there and better than him (drawn by rating like a roll; fewer than three better are filled with the next best, tagged "Better" or "Next best" under the card); a red shirt costs nothing and names the best man there. A club (Craig, 20 Oct): the season's clubs with cards still to come, never yours, in table order with a player count (`.se-clubgrid`); tap one for three of its players on the current date (`clubAt`), drawn by rating like a roll (`stream(seed, "scoutclub", ...)`, `roll.scoutClub`, `GAME.scoutedClub`). Scouts never bring a purple or legend card, gold at most (Craig, 21 Oct, build 138: Greg's five purples in a season; measured, a position scout on the weakest shirt of a built-up XI returned purples 35% of the time against 3% from a roll, a mean 17 points over the holder, and the test policies never scout, so no balance run had seen it; now 0% and 13 points). A Club X career from season 2 draws purple 1.5% and legend 0.5% (`RULES.levelOddsCareer` 38 / 24 / 36 / 1.5 / 0.5, `drawCard` by `st.career > 1`; the guaranteed legend stays); Classic, the Daily and a career's first season keep 3% and 1% (Classic's average player sits at 4.3% titles against Craig's 5%, so a global cut would undershoot it). (6) **Defenders**: `SLOTW` reweighted (GK 1.8, CB 1.6, full backs 0.9, wing backs 0.7, ST 1.0, wingers 0.85: a rating point on a CB worth 0.24 points a season against a striker's 0.36, was 0.17 and 0.40) and +2 on every defender card at build time (`DEF_LIFT` in build-data.py, Craig: so defenders look valued); the match model refitted (`RULES.match` base 1.279, att 0.0408, mid 0.0055). (7) **Every season puts one legend on the table** (`legendRoll`, `newRoll`: if none by a seeded roll between the 3rd and the 3rd-last, that roll's first card is one); odds purple 3%, legend 1%. Amber is 90%, anything else red at 70%. Pearce (CB, LB): green CB, LB, LWB; amber CDM, LM, LW; red on the right. Salah (RW, ST): green RW, ST; amber RM, LW, CAM. Cards show the main position; the big card lists the others. While a card is selected or dragged on the squad or transfer pitch (and the Club X draft), every shirt gets a 1px rounded ring in green, amber or red for him 3.5px out from it (`se-pf-*`, `--ring`; Craig, 13 Oct, `design/prototype/fit-ring-glow.css`, SVG masks drawn 1:1 for the 50 x 65 pitch card so the line is even and the point never clipped; the transfer pitch's 57.5 x 75 cards have their own set for a 64.5 x 82 ring box, `.tr-field.tr-big` in season.css, made by the same method, Craig 14 Oct): round a card the gap inside the ring glows in the fit colour from behind it (`.se-cardwrap::before`; nothing is laid over the card), round an empty shirt (`.se-emptywrap`) the shirt is also filled faintly. A card on the pitch shows the shirt it is in on its tab, the tab coloured green (one of his positions), amber (next to them) or red (out of position) (`tab` and `cls: 'fit-…'` in `D2T.cards.player`; Craig, 13 Oct), with a soft patch of light in the same colour on the grass under it where its shadow sits (Craig, 14 Oct; `.se-slot:has(.d2c.fit-…)::after`). Only green shirts bounce while a card is selected or dragged (`seBest`, `seArmed`); amber and red stay still. Tabs off the pitch (bench cards, the drag ghost) are neutral, dark `#26262C` with white text.
- Matches: Poisson goals from attack v defence and midfield gaps, fitted to every real result (`tests/fit-match.js`, `RULES.match`; each match's goals come from `stream(seed, "match", gw)`, so only the seed and the XI decide a result). The fit is Newton's method on a home and an away intercept, `att` and `mid` (13 Oct: `base`, `home` and `away` are one parameter too many, and the old gradient descent never converged, leaving 3.4 goals a game against 2.7 real and twice the real share of 5-goal margins); on real fixtures the model now gives 2.70 goals a game, 23.7% draws and 2.2% of matches won by 5 or more (real 2.69, 25.6%, 2.0%), checked in `tests/engine.test.js`. Opponents use their real squad's best XI minus anyone you've signed.
- Balance targets (Craig, 15 Oct; to do once the player data session's extra purple and legend cards, and any 2 to 5 point boosts for players in the 80s, are in): a relegated side should win the league about 5% of the time overall (greedy `calibrate-all` was 1.1%), higher finishes should generally be more reachable, and the hardest, Derby 2007/08 (11 pts), about 2% (0 in 400 on 15 Oct). Not evened out: weaker squads stay harder, no handicap by points gap.
- Raids: signing a player lowers his club's strength from that date; each of its later real wins or draws can be downgraded with chance 3 x the strength lost (`otherResults`). Everyone else's results stay as history.
- Table: other clubs' matches count on their real dates; your 38 are simulated.

## Interface (src/app)

- Built from the design prototype (`design/prototype`, spec in `design/UI_SPEC.md`). `core/app.js` is the shell: `D2T.screen(name, def)`, hash routes, `D2T.state` saved as `d2t-v2`, `D2T.sheet`, `D2T.topbar`, logos as masks. `core/game.js` (`D2T.game`) is the only bridge to the engine and data: screens never call `D2TE` directly. `core/season.js` holds the in-run helpers, `core/boot.js` starts sound and the router.
- Screens: intro (green, then to black), home (black), then the small white D2T logo and the account circle: howto, account, league, stats, spin (club roll), table (historic reveal), squad (pitch, formations, drag to swap), transfer (the roll, then the pitch above a tray of the three cards), fixtures, match, results, standings, summary.
- Match flow (Craig, 14 Oct): the fixtures screen's main button is Kick off: it decides the match in the engine there and then (`GAME.play`, so a refresh can never change a result) and opens the match screen, which plays it out at once (no preview screen). The match screen shows the scoreboard and clock, the scorers panel under it (home scorers right-aligned on the left, the ball, away scorers on the right, with minutes; `SE.scorers`, filled in live as each goal goes in), and the commentary feed, newest line at the top, whose first line is the ratings panel (Your XI rating, the opponent's rating and real finish, and the note on how the result is played out): it sits under the kick-off line and moves down as the commentary runs (Craig, 14 Oct); Skip to end, then at full time the result chip beside the clock (Win · +3 points) with the crowd's verdict (`result`), and See results; half a second after full time it moves on to the results by itself, which show the final score (Craig, 14 Oct). The settled match is only for looking back: the results' back arrow opens it (`run.review`), and a refresh mid-match goes on to the results. The results screen (results.js, route `#results`): your match highlighted at the top with the score, scorers and the result chip, then the gameweek's other scores grouped by date like the fixtures screen (`GAME.gwResults`, raids applied; Craig, 14 Oct: no edge on raided rows, instead a dot in your club's colour for each player you signed from a club this season, in a row at the far left of the row for the home side and the far right for the away side, a Club X career's later seasons leaving out the XI carried in, `GAME.signedFrom`, `.se-fx-dots`, with a key under the note; the fixtures screen shows the same dots and key before the game, Craig 15 Oct), League table across the right. The standings screen (`#standings`, also results.js): the table rolling from where it stood before the gameweek to after it (the old full-time screen's animated table, `tableMove`, the thump on your row), Continue hands the run on (`GAME.afterMatch`: a transfer window, the next gameweek or the summary, so recording, January windows and roll windows come in the same order as before). The old full-time screen held only the score, the scorers, the result chip, a note and the after-gameweek table; the chip and the crowd sound moved to the match screen, the scorers to the match and results screens, the table to the standings screen. `run.match` holds the gameweek on show until Continue and `run.step` which of match, results and standings is up (`GAME.stepFor`, `MATCH_STEPS`; all three allowed while a match is on, so back steps through them); `run.watch` is set at Kick off and taken by the match screen as it mounts, so a refresh mid-match goes on to the results, a refresh on the results or the table lands on them. The mid-season squad screen is skipped in the normal flow: after a transfer window (`GAME.signAt`, `GAME.pass`, transfer.js) and between matches the run goes straight to the fixtures (Kick off); the squad stays before the season (the real squad and bench, the first XI, the formation, Transfer window or Start the season), at the end of a draft and a career's next season (pre-season), and mid-season by the fixtures' back arrow (to rearrange or change shape; its own bar then has a back arrow to the fixtures and a Fixtures main button, and a refresh keeps you there, `run.step` squad).
- In-run screens only open when the run has reached them (`GAME.allowed`, `GAME.stepFor`); spins and the table reveal never replay (`run.spun`, `run.tableSeen`). Matches are decided in the engine before the animation, so a refresh can't change a result.
- Squad bench (Craig, 15 Oct): the bench cards are the roll's full cards, 114 wide as they roll in (80 on phones under 760px tall, where the pitch may drop to 280px), each with his green positions in grey under it as in the transfer tray (`.se-sub-pos`, "CDM/CM"); a picked card shows no caret, and a picked card's tab turns white with dark text.
- Squad: the whole real squad is on the bench before the season and a formation change picks a new best XI; once the season starts only the XI is left. In Dare2Dream the squad screen first shows empty shirts (pick the shape, then Start the draft; before the draft its bar is home, a greyed respin and Start the draft, Craig 13 Oct); the draft runs on the transfer screen (`GAME.inDraft`, `GAME.draftInfo`, `GAME.draftPick`; engine `draftPick`, `draftSlot`, `draftFormation`).
- Transfer: before the spin the roll header (Craig, 14 Oct; claude.ai/artifact/Vv39dhAkYKfbAaLiCWUkwT; `headTop`, `headBanner` in `D2T.roll.mount`, `GAME.rollTrack`) shows the season's track, one segment per roll (done in the accent at 45%, the current one lit, Summer and January segments still to come a shade lighter, SUMMER and JANUARY under their three where those windows exist; a Club X first season has 14 and no Summer), the kicker "Transfer 7 of 17", "Who comes in?", in the Summer or January window a banner ("The Summer window is open", SUMMER WINDOW, three dots filled to this roll, "2 of 3"), and the sub only the fixture ("Before GW13 against Middlesbrough (H). Sign one or pass."); the accent is the mode's colour (Classic the club's `text`, Daily white, Club X green; `D2T.modeWord`). The draft keeps its own header. The transfer pitch's cards are 15% bigger than the squad's, 57.5 x 75 (`PITCH_W` in transfer.js), each line nudged up or down the pitch on top of its formation position (`nudge`: GK +8, back line -4, CDM -8, other midfield -4, forwards -2; 5-3-2's CBs -10; 4-2-3-1 back line 0, CDMs -2 and 6px wider, CAM and wingers -4, ST -6), the keeper never closer than 4px to the goal line and no ring over a touchline (`place`); a pitch under 400px tall (375-wide phones) keeps the 50 x 65 cards and plain positions (`.tr-big`, a ResizeObserver). Tap the selected card again for the big card (Craig, 13 Oct; option B in `design/prototype/player-detail.html`, `D2T.cards.info`): the card at about two thirds size (236 wide on a 393 phone), then This season (border tinted in the card's level colour) and PL career side by side, each games, goals, assists and clean sheets in the same spots for every player, then a strip with his positions (main filled in the level colour) and his seasons and club crests in career order (4, then +n). The overlay scrolls when it doesn't fit (`scroll` in `openLarge`); scrolling or tapping the panels never closes it, the card, the X or outside does. The squad screen's big card is the same. - Team rating is the average of the 11 effective ratings (each cut to 90% or 70% when out of position).
- Long surnames shrink to fit the card (`fitName` in `core/cards.js`), never an ellipsis unless at 55%.
- Accounts: optional sign-in with the same email and 4-digit PIN accounts as Tenaball, on Tenaball's server (`tenaball.crw84.workers.dev`, which allows 404craig.github.io). Runs and records stay on the phone for now.

- Build order of CSS and JS is the list at the top of `scripts/build.py`; add new screens there.
- Loading (12 and 15 Oct): the page streams in parts so the intro starts in the first ~40 KB (gzipped): a small head (13 KB gzipped, inside the first round trip: base.css and front.css only, `CSS_FIRST`; the fonts load without holding up the first frame; icons as files), the short logo (`LOGOS_FIRST`), the shell (`SHELL` in build.py: app.js and intro.js, which start the intro), the intro's other logos and the outline emblem (`D2T.setLogos`, `D2T.introArt` puts them on the intro's pieces), the rest of the CSS, the data (game data as a `JSON.parse` string), the rest of the game (boot.js calls `D2T.ready()`; the intro waits for it with `D2T.whenReady` before going home), then the sound clips (`D2TSound.clipsLoaded`). ## Data

- `data/source/pl_results.csv` and `pl_players.csv` come from Tenaball (two sources each, see the READMEs there).
- Icons and honours (Craig, 15 Oct; `docs/cards/ICONS.md`): `data/source/honours.json` holds every season's PFA Team of the Year, PFA and FWA Player of the Year, PL Player of the Season and Golden Glove, parsed from the Wikipedia pages it names (the Golden Boot is the data's own top scorer); `data/source/research/icons_*.json` are the published all-time lists per position with their sources; `tools/build_icons.py` turns them and Craig's list into `data/source/icons.json` (consensus, verified peak seasons with evidence). Rerun it, then build-data, after changing any of them. The research files' own peak guesses are never used (from memory).
- Ratings: `data/ratings/rated_fit_*.json` from the D2T player data work, every season 1992/93 to 2025/26 (method in `docs/cards/RATINGS_TEST.md`, built by `tools/rate_v5.py` and `tools/fit_panel.py` on the player data branch). The fallback model in `build-data.py` only applies if a season's file is missing (`src: "f"`, shown as provisional).
- Preferred positions: `data/source/positions.json` from Transfermarkt's position for every Premier League game (`tools/fetch_tm_positions.py`, cache in `docs/cards/feed/tm/`), matched to Tenaball player-seasons and reduced to a main position plus up to three others by `tools/build_positions.py` and `tools/positions_rule.py`; checked against the Premier League feed's formation slots from 2015/16 (`tools/fetch_slots.py`, `tools/check_positions_feed.py`). Method and results in `docs/cards/POSITIONS.md`. Craig's own calls override the data for every season of a player (`data/source/positions_overrides.json`, applied by build-data.py; 15 Oct: Gianfranco Zola CAM, then ST).
- Club accents in `data/source/clubs.json` (`accent`, `ink`, `text`); every `text` passes 4.5:1 on black.
- Crests are trademarks: fine for the family version, replace before any public release.
- White crests on the club's own card (Craig, 12 and 14 Oct): where a crest is all, or nearly all, the card's colour it is shown white on every club card (Classic and Daily club spin, Unbeaten Run), via `crestCard` in `data/source/clubs.json` (a white file `<club>_white.png` in `assets/crests/72` and `128`; `crestCard` in core/game.js). The list: Nottingham Forest, Liverpool, Sheffield Wed. Checked on 14 Oct for every club (share of a crest's pixels close to its card colour, then each card looked at): Blackpool's orange ring lettering fades on the orange card but its arms read, Oldham's navy owl reads on blue, so neither is whitened.
