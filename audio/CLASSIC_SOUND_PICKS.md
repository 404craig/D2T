# DROP2TOP Classic sounds: final picks (13 Oct 2026)

Craig chose these on the Classic sound kit (claude.ai/artifact/5K8i5k3cEhWwVX1pDNbr4P). Everything not already in the Soft style was redone softer and quieter, and his notes were applied. Recorded clips for the game are the softened files in `audio/soft/`; code sounds are the recipes in `audio/classic_soundkit.html` (the `CUES` list). Small actions marked muted play through a low-passed channel capped below the volume setting.

Sources: CODE = made in code, ELEVENLABS = softened ElevenLabs clip, MIX = both.
