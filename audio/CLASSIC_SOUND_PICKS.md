# DROP2TOP Classic sounds: final picks (13 Oct 2026)

Craig chose these on the Classic sound kit (claude.ai/artifact/5K8i5k3cEhWwVX1pDNbr4P).

Rules from Craig for every game sound:
- Soft, subtle and quiet. Anything not already in the Soft style was redone softer and quieter.
- No glass or tin sounds: no glassy notes, bells, chimes, pings or metal clinks. Ticks are muffled taps; reels land on felt thuds, not metal latches.
- Small actions (back, tabs, panels, tips, card picks) are muted: a low-passed channel capped below the volume setting.

Recorded clips for the game are the softened files in `audio/soft/`; code sounds are the recipes in `audio/classic_soundkit.html` (the `CUES` list).

Sources: CODE = made in code, ELEVENLABS = softened ElevenLabs clip, MIX = both.
